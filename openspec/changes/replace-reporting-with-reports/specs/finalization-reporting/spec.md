## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Reporting work is queued by status
**Reason**: The reporting queue is unused and is replaced by the Reports workspace, which generates downloadable period reports on demand.
**Migration**: Finalization revisions continue to receive their reporting-destination status rows, and those records are retained. The queue view, its filters, and its Mark entered / Mark not required actions are removed. The retained status records are reserved for a future Fire export that marks classes exported.
