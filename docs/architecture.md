# Architecture

## Finalization reporting

Finalization is a class-level transaction. It locks the class, revalidates the roster, captures an encrypted snapshot of class, instructor, attendance, agency, credit, attestation, and reporting-requirement facts, then creates the next immutable revision. Database uniqueness on `(class_id, revision_number)` and update/delete-blocking rules preserve revision lineage.

Reopens are append-only records tied to the prior revision. They return a class to the open correction workflow; completing and finalizing it creates a later revision rather than modifying history. Reporting status rows are destination-specific and are independently queued as `pending` or `needs_correction`.

Assigned instructors can finalize and reopen only their assigned classes. Training Majors and Administrators also have lifecycle access and are the only roles allowed to update Fire, KBEMS, and FEMA reporting destinations.

## Official roster PDFs

KFS-1A October 2023 is rendered server-side from decrypted finalization-revision snapshots and returned directly to authorized Training Majors and Administrators; the KBEMS-E16 form is retired. Generated bytes, object-storage records, and artifact metadata are not retained. Staff clients receive only the same-origin authorized download response, never snapshot facts.

Template mappings are owned with their checked-in source PDF in `src/modules/official-roster-pdfs`. Any revised official form requires a new asset/version/checksum, mapping review, representative and overflow fixture review, and approval before use. KFS-1B individual training notices are appended to the Fire roster for participants outside the host agency; FEMA remains out of scope. The KFS-1A lead-instructor signature and attendee initials are populated from the revision attestation; the assisting signature remains physically blank.
